Pensa is installed

Do you opt in to sharing anonymous data to help a high schooler's research project?

More details

Pensa is a research project by a high schooler, measuring how common persuasion techniques are across shops, and how often people add an item after seeing one. That needs reports from real shopping.

What a report says

  • the shop's domain, for example shein.com
  • which technique appeared
  • where in checkout it appeared
  • whether you clicked Add to Cart
  • the date, never a time

What it never says

  • your name, email, or any ID for you or your browser
  • the page or the product, only the shop
  • anything you searched or typed
  • any price, total, or what you spent
  • anything at all from a page that is not a shop

Reports are counted and stored as totals, never as a list of what one person did. Nothing is sold, and no advertising or analytics company is involved. You can read the exact reports waiting to be sent, and change your mind whenever you like. Switching it off deletes anything not yet sent.
