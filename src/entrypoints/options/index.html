Anonymous statistics

A count says “someone saw a countdown, on a travel site, in this hour.” It carries no web address, no page text, no prices, no account, and no exact time. Counts are sent on a six-hour timer rather than when something is found, so the timing says nothing either — and a batch is held back until at least 20 other reports share its shape, because a count that only you could have produced is not anonymous however few fields it has.
